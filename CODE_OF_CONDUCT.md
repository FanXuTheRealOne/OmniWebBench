# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in OmniWebBench a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, sexual identity and orientation, or technical choices.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific and evidence-led.
- Critique benchmark methods and results, not people.
- Disclose conflicts of interest and benchmark contamination.
- Respect responsible-disclosure and private-data boundaries.
- Accept that reproducibility claims require reproducible evidence.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, doxxing, publishing private information, deliberate benchmark sabotage, hidden-test extraction and knowingly fraudulent result submissions are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct issues privately to repository maintainers. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban repeated or severe violations. Enforcement decisions should document the behavior and proportional response while protecting reporter privacy.

This policy is adapted from Contributor Covenant 2.1.
